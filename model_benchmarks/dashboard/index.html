Why FarmMind Selected MobileNetV3 over ResNet-50

While ResNet-50 achieves a marginal +1.0% higher raw classification accuracy, empirical edge profiling proves that it is unviable for offline deployment on budget farmer smartphones in rural India.

5.5x Faster
25.1 ms vs. 138.4 ms

MobileNetV3 provides instant real-time shutter feedback on ARM CPUs without thermal throttling.

5.6x Smaller
4.3 MB vs. 24.2 MB

TFLite package downloads in seconds even on unstable 2G/3G connectivity in remote farmland.

4.3x Less RAM
38 MB vs. 165 MB

Zero Out-Of-Memory (OOM) crashes on low-end Android handsets with 2 GB – 3 GB total RAM.

Conclusion: MobileNetV3 achieves the optimal Pareto frontier between accuracy (94.2%), latency (25.1 ms), and storage footprint (4.3 MB).
